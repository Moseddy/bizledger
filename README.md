# BizLedger

BizLedger is a bookkeeping and business financial management application designed to help Nigerian small-business owners record, monitor and understand their daily business finances.

The goal is to provide business owners with a simple, reliable and accessible platform for managing their business financial records without depending on complicated accounting software.

## Project Objectives

BizLedger aims to help small businesses:

- Record daily sales and income.
- Track business expenses and expenditure.
- Monitor customer debts and outstanding payments.
- Track supplier debts and liabilities.
- Separate personal and business transactions.
- Generate daily, weekly and monthly financial reports.
- Monitor business profitability.
- Export financial records to PDF and Excel.
- Understand business performance through dashboards and financial summaries.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js
- **Programming Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Supabase
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Authentication:** Supabase Auth
- **Version Control:** Git and GitHub

## Development Philosophy

BizLedger is being developed incrementally, with an emphasis on:

- Data security and privacy.
- Accurate financial calculations.
- Simple and intuitive user experience.
- Reliable database architecture.
- Multi-business support.
- Affordable infrastructure and open-source technologies.

## Development Status

Currently under active development.

## Project Vision

To make proper financial record-keeping accessible to Nigerian small businesses, helping business owners make better decisions through accurate and understandable financial information.
